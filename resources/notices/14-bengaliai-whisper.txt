Bengali.AI Whisper medium for Bangla (the Bangla speech model, ggml conversion by WinShades)
https://huggingface.co/bengaliAI/tugstugi_bengaliai-asr_whisper-medium (revision
da605cc1bd2f60a18d8e440e977ddfa921a88e63): the tugstugi team's first-place model of the Bengali.AI
speech recognition competition, fine-tuned from OpenAI Whisper medium (MIT licence, see the OpenAI
Whisper entry). Copyright the Bengali.AI authors and the tugstugi team.

The file Flown downloads when you ask it to (the Bangla model, used only for Bengali captions) is
ggml-bengaliai-whisper-medium-q5_0.bin from https://huggingface.co/winshades/bengaliai-whisper-medium-ggml
at commit 11cf5cbd282e0e46a761093939952f74906ddce1 (539,257,671 bytes, SHA-256
2c64c7f1e2c8f2417ed1fe5b78c7238933c00a2cdc2ef33e7087a95475256605), checked against its SHA-256
before use. It is a modified version, as its card states: the weights were converted to the ggml
format with whisper.cpp's models/convert-h5-to-ggml.py at 16-bit precision and compressed to q5_0
with whisper-quantize; nothing was retrained. It is not part of the installer.
