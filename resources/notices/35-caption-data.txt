Caption data (Captions Studio)
https://www.unicode.org/Public/18.0.0/emoji/emoji-test.txt
Flown's caption emoji list (src/shared/captions/emojiList.ts) is generated from Unicode's
emoji-test.txt, version 18.0 (671,655 bytes, SHA-256
8f3735cda1f92a779d78af67cf86066bb1f07143dc22f2ac29394d9bc57ab21a): the fully-qualified emoji, their
names and groups, limited to those the bundled Noto Color Emoji font draws. Modified: filtered and
reformatted.

The emoji keywords of other languages (src/shared/captions/emojiData.ts) come from the Unicode
Common Locale Data Repository (CLDR) 49.0.0 emoji annotations (npm package cldr-annotations-full
49.0.0), and the Bangla and Hindi number words (src/shared/captions/numberData.ts) from CLDR
49.0.0's rule-based number format data (npm package cldr-rbnf 49.0.0). Modified: selected,
filtered and reformatted.
