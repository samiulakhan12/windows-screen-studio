llama.cpp and its ggml, at llama.cpp commit d81235049384534c167caea52b85a694f6103d14
https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp (vendored by the Rust crate llama-cpp-sys-2 0.1.161, built
from llama-cpp-rs commit e7af607ebd5ec2932709ef918c17cca2f9b0fce4)
Compiled into resources/native/ssw-llm.exe (the text helper of AI on this PC). Its HTTP client
and the rest of llama.cpp's `common` library are compiled by the crate but not linked in.
