Electron and Chromium
https://www.electronjs.org/ and https://www.chromium.org/
Flown runs on Electron, which includes Chromium, Node.js and their third-party components. The
Electron licence (MIT) is reproduced below with the other packages. The licences of Chromium and
of every component it includes are installed with Flown as LICENSES.chromium.html, next to
Flown.exe, and Electron's own LICENSE file is installed there too.
