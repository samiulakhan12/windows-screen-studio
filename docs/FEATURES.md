# Feature list

Legend: **✓** shipped · *roadmap* = planned. "Windows-unverified" marks behaviour that is implemented
and tested on Linux / with stand-ins, but has not yet been run on a real Windows PC.

## Recording
- ✓ Pick a full screen, an **area** (drag a rectangle on a transparent overlay; move/resize handles, size readout, Enter/Esc), or a single window. Source thumbnails refresh on demand.
- ✓ **Single-window recordings keep every cursor effect**: the native helper records just that window without the Windows cursor (`record --window`) and reports where the window is as it moves, resizes or is minimised; cursor samples and clicks are mapped into the window, so auto zoom, click ripples, follow-cursor zooms and the smooth cursor (with real cursor shapes) all work. A resized window is fitted into the original frame with the same rule everywhere. Clicks on another app's window covering the recorded one are ignored (and the cursor is hidden there); dragging the window keeps the cursor aligned; windows spanning monitors with different scaling are mapped correctly. Without native window capture the window is recorded by Chromium while the helper only watches its position (`watch-window`): auto zoom and ripples still work, with the Windows cursor in the video. *Windows-unverified.*
- ✓ Microphone (device picker, falls back to the default device with a notice if the chosen one is gone) and **system audio** (Windows loopback). The mixed track is kept, and each source is also saved as its own file (`mic.m4a`, `system.m4a`), lined up with the video by matching the sound itself. A source that fails to save never fails the recording, and survives crash recovery, *Finish later* and *Retry*. *System-audio stems Windows-unverified.*
- ✓ Webcam recorded as its own track for the PiP bubble; its start offset is measured from the first frames.
- ✓ 3-2-1 countdown, floating always-on-top control bar (excluded from the capture) with timer, pause/resume, stop and discard (with confirmation). A hotkey (default `Ctrl+Shift+2`) stops; if another app owns it the bar says so.
- ✓ Recording options on Home, saved in the app settings: 30 or 60 fps (the editing proxy keeps the rate); hide desktop icons while recording (Windows; restored on stop, cancel, crash, quit or next start; *Windows-unverified*); auto-pause when idle (3–60 s, "Paused (idle)" on the bar, resumes on the next input); custom hotkeys for stop / pause-resume / zoom here (default `Ctrl+Shift+2` / `Ctrl+Shift+F9` / `Ctrl+Shift+F10`, never a shortcut apps rely on such as Redo or a command palette), checked for conflicts and for combos another app owns.
- ✓ Live camera preview on Home (mirrored; released the moment recording starts).
- ✓ **Live camera bubble while recording**: the webcam shows in a round or rounded bubble on the screen as you record, kept out of the capture (Windows 10 2004+). Drag it anywhere or snap it to a corner; the corners it visits become camera positions over time in the edit (turn off *camera follows the bubble* to keep one corner). The recording bar can hide and show it; the camera keeps recording. *Windows-unverified.*
- ✓ **Speaker notes (teleprompter)**: paste a script or bullet points on Home (*Speaker notes*, then *Add notes…*); while recording they show in a small window near the webcam that only you see: kept out of the video and out of screen sharing (Windows 10 2004+), never taking focus from the app you record, and clicks, wheel and hotkeys on it never become recorded input. It appears with the countdown, scrolls at your reading speed (each line takes as long as reading its words; 60–220 words a minute), pauses with the recording, and goes away when it stops. A reading line at 30 % with the read text dimmed; bullets, and *[cues in brackets]* that are shown but not counted. Controls on the window (back, play / pause, forward, speed, text size, a resize grip, drag to move), the mouse wheel and Ctrl + wheel, hotkeys (`Ctrl+Shift+F7` play / pause, `Ctrl+Shift+F6` back, `Ctrl+Shift+F8` forward, changeable in the Notes dialog), and a show / hide button on the recording bar. *Rehearse* opens it from Home, fully usable from the keyboard. *Draft script* turns bullet points into sentences to read aloud with your OpenAI, Gemini or Claude key (applied only if the notes did not change meanwhile; *Restore my notes* brings them back). Auto-pause counts scrolling notes as activity. The notes are kept between sessions, and a copy goes with each recording (`script.txt`, and *Your script* in the editor's Captions tab with *Use as notes again*). On older Windows the notes appear only outside what you record (never in window recordings), locked in place, and Home says so. Focus, capture exclusion, Alt+Tab, mixed-DPI placement and the wheel over an inactive window are *Windows-unverified* (manual checks in `docs/design/teleprompter.md` §7.4).
- ✓ **Recording bar**: recorded time, microphone mute, hide camera, hide speaker notes, pause / resume, **restart** (discard the take and record again) and discard with a confirm step, and stop; it shows hotkey problems and the auto-pause state.
- ✓ **Recording presets** on Home: save the current setup (source kind, frame rate, microphone / system audio / camera and their devices, countdown, auto-pause, desktop icons, keyboard activity) under a name and pick it again later; a device that is gone falls back to the default with a notice.
- ✓ "Zoom here" marks and typing bursts for auto zoom (only *when* typing happened, never which keys; the "Record keyboard activity" toggle covers shortcuts and typing).
- ✓ **Native Windows capture** (`ssw-capture.exe`, Windows Graphics Capture + Media Foundation): screens and areas are recorded **without the Windows cursor**, encoded on the GPU, with paused time cut out. Chromium records the audio. Any failure falls back to Chromium capture automatically. *Windows-unverified.*
- ✓ Cursor position, clicks (clicks on the app's own windows and outside the recorded display/area are ignored), cursor shape (native capture) and **shortcut keystrokes** (combos with Ctrl/Alt/Win and special keys only; plain typing is never recorded; can be turned off on Home).
- ✓ Recordings are **streamed to disk** while recording (no size limit, flat memory) and are never discarded once capture has started: a crashed recorder, a failed conversion or a closed window leaves recoverable files, listed under *Unfinished recordings* (Recover / Retry / Show folder / Delete). Processing shows a percentage and can be finished later.
- ✓ One recording at a time (double clicks are ignored), single app instance, friendly errors with a link to Windows privacy settings.
- ✓ Microphone level meter on Home; if system audio cannot start, the recording continues without it and says so; if the native helper stops on its own (e.g. a monitor is unplugged) the recording stops, keeps everything and explains why. Native recordings are written as fragmented MP4, so even a helper crash leaves a playable file.
- *roadmap*: cropping areas inside the native helper (today the whole monitor is captured and cropped afterwards).

## Motion engine
- ✓ **Auto zoom** generated from click clusters (zoom in just before the click, hold, ease out), aimed at the click itself even when the cursor arrives just before it; regenerate keeps your edited zooms.
- ✓ Zoom camera **follows the cursor** with spring smoothing, clamped to the frame; or a **fixed point** picked on the preview.
- ✓ **Smooth cursor**: spring-smoothed path, adjustable smoothness and size (DPI-independent), real cursor shapes from native capture. Only offered when the Windows cursor is not already in the video.
- ✓ Click ripples (work with either cursor), hide cursor when idle.
- ✓ **Motion blur** on camera moves (zoom and pan), scaled by clip speed; static frames cost nothing extra.
- ✓ **Zoom easing** (smooth, snappy, bouncy, linear) and transition per zoom, or as the project's Zoom style (Apply to all).
- ✓ **Connected zooms** pan straight from one to the next without zooming out; *Pan to next zoom* joins them; linked zooms are drawn joined on the timeline.
- ✓ **Region zoom**: draw, move or resize a rectangle on the preview; the zoom frames exactly it.
- ✓ **Pull back**: levels 0.5–1× shrink the recording card (crisp corners and shadow) to show more background.
- ✓ **Auto zoom settings** (level, grouping, lead-in, hold, right clicks, typing, zoom-here marks, while scrolling) regenerate automatic zooms live.
- ✓ **Ignore stray clicks**: every recorded click is a marker on the clip track (hollow when ignored, dashed when Flown ignored it). Select one and press `Delete`, use *Ignore click* / *Use click* in Zoom › Selected click, *Ignore this click* on the automatic zoom it made ("Made for the click at 0:04.2"), or the Recorded clicks list in Zoom › Auto zoom. An ignored click starts no automatic zoom and has no click effect, no click sound and no smart speed activity; only automatic zooms change, so zooms you added or edited stay. New recordings start with obvious stray clicks ignored (the click that focuses the app in the first 0.6 s, a click right before stopping, a click made while the cursor flew past); *Find stray clicks* does the same for older recordings and *Use all clicks* undoes it. Every change is one undo step, and `cursor.json` is never changed.
- ✓ **Straighten cursor moves** (Cursor › Motion): wobbly moves between two resting points become a straight, smoothly eased glide in the preview, the export, the follow-cursor camera and motion blur. Drags, loops and moves across a click keep their recorded path. Select a range (`Shift`+drag on the clip track) to *Straighten* it, keep it *As recorded*, or *Reset* it to follow the option; straightened moves show as thin bars on the clip track. Off by default.
- ✓ **Follow scrolling**: mouse-wheel scrolling is recorded. During a long scroll (1.5 s and 4 notches or more) auto zoom shows the whole page, then zooms back in where the scrolling stopped; shorter scrolls hold the zoom. *While scrolling* (Zoom › Auto zoom) picks Zoom out, Hold or Ignore; long scrolls show as hatched bands on the zoom track. Recordings made before scrolling was captured work exactly as before. Mouse-wheel notches, releases and positions on Windows touchpads and mixed-DPI setups are *Windows-unverified*.
- ✓ **Typing zoom**: a click into a field and the typing that follows become one zoom that holds through the whole burst (and short pauses), aimed at where you clicked rather than at the idle mouse.
- ✓ **3D tilt**: the recording shown as a tilted card in perspective (presets plus angle and perspective sliders), standing still, leaning gently toward the cursor, or flattening while zoomed in. Drawn with WebGL in preview and export; part of style presets and the default style. The preview shows the flat frame while you edit something on it, so handles line up.
- ✓ **Cursor styles**: Auto (recorded shapes), macOS-like, Windows-like, large white/black, or a custom PNG/JPG/WebP/SVG image; highlight halo; ripple / ring / pulse click effects in any colour; click sounds (preview and export); loop home for seamless GIFs; tilt and motion trail.

## Editor
- ✓ **Undo / redo** for every edit (`Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+Y` / `Ctrl+Shift+Z`, toolbar buttons); drags and slider moves are one step each.
- ✓ **Cuts and speed**: trim handles, split at playhead (`S`), cut a clip or a Shift-dragged range (`Delete`), restore cuts, speed presets 0.25×–8× per clip or range. Preview playback skips cuts and plays each clip at its speed with pitch preserved; the transport shows output time.
- ✓ **Freeze frame** (`F`, or Clip tab › Freeze frame): holds the frame under the playhead for 3 s (0.5–30 s). *Pause the sound* inserts the still into the video while the recording waits and the music plays on; *Keep the sound playing* holds the picture while the voice, camera and captions go on. Freezes are hatched blocks on the clip track: drag to move, drag the right edge for the length, `Delete` removes. Preview and export agree frame for frame, and chapters, subtitles and click sounds follow the added time. A freeze whose frame is cut stays (hollow) and comes back with the cut. Smart speed and Remove silences never cut a freeze.
- ✓ **J / K / L and preview speed**: `L` plays forward and `J` backwards; tap again for 2×, 4×, 8×; `K` pauses (hold `K` and tap `J`/`L` to step one frame). A speed menu next to the time plays the preview at 0.5×, 1×, 1.5× or 2× (`<` / `>`) for reviewing long recordings; a badge shows the shuttle rate. Preview only: the project and the export never change. Reverse plays without sound; away from 1× the sound plays only while it runs between 0.5× and 4×.
- ✓ **Zoom segments**: add (`Z` / double-click, at the default level), move, resize, delete; level (presets 0.75×–3×, keys `1`–`4` / `0`), follow cursor, fixed point or region. Adding never deletes or shortens existing zooms.
- ✓ **Text annotations** on their own track (`T` / double-click): text (multi-line), caption pill or title style, size, position by dragging on the preview.
- ✓ **Keystroke overlay**: keycap pills for recorded shortcuts, top or bottom, adjustable size.
- ✓ Background (10 gradient presets, solid colour, custom image), padding, corner radius, shadow; aspect ratios Auto, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3.
- ✓ **Crop the recording** (Style › Frame › Crop recording…, or `C`): hide the taskbar, browser tabs or a screen edge after recording. The tool shows the whole recording with a draggable rectangle (8 handles, Free / Original / 16:9 / 4:3 / 1:1 / 9:16), *Hide taskbar* and *Hide browser bar* presets, Reset, `Esc` to cancel and `Enter` to apply, in one undo step. Nothing is re-encoded. Zooms, cursor, click effects, shapes, blur and spotlights stay on their content; clicks in the hidden part make no zoom, ripple or sound. With the Auto aspect the video takes the cropped shape, and the export warns when it would be upscaled.
- ✓ **Vertical video that follows the action** (NW-05, `docs/design/vertical-video.md`): in 9:16, 1:1 and other frames narrower than the recording, *Follow the action* (Style › Vertical framing) fills the screen's part of the frame and pans a view across the recording instead of shrinking the whole desktop into a strip. The view is planned once, on the PC, from where the cursor comes to rest and where it is going, clicks, typing, drawn shapes, scrolls and zooms: it holds still most of the time and moves in calm, eased pans that start as the cursor starts moving and arrive in time for the click, never across a cut; flicks, jitter and short visits do not move it. Zooms work inside the view, gentler than in 16:9 and limited so the picture stays sharp. *How close* (1×–2×), *Movement* (Calm, Balanced, Lively), the camera in a band on top or at the bottom of a tall frame (or its bubble), *Keep text clear of app buttons* (captions, key presses, the logo and lower-third titles placed where TikTok, Reels and Shorts leave the video free), and a view that rests where you put it for recordings without cursor data. Steer it with *Hold the view here* (`R`), by dragging the picture, with a zoom's fixed point or with *Ignore click*. While following, the device frame and the 3D tilt are left out (kept for 16:9), and click effects grow with the picture as the cursor does. Old projects, including 9:16 and 1:1 ones, draw and export exactly as before until following is turned on. Screenshots: `docs/screenshots/vertical-video-*.png`.
- ✓ **Safe-zone preview** (`G`, or *Safe zones* under a tall preview): shades where TikTok, Reels and Shorts put their buttons and captions (all three, or one app), with a legend. It is drawn over the preview only, never into an export, a thumbnail or the clipboard; the choice is remembered on this PC.
- ✓ **Duplicate, Make a vertical version, Make a square version** (X-04; the copy button right of the recording's name, or the command palette): the copy is a new project in the library, made from the editor's current state, and opens with *Open original*. A vertical or square version follows the action and moves text and images once into the new frame; a vertical one puts the camera in a band on top and the text into the area the apps leave free, off the band. The recording's media are hard-linked where that is safe (copied otherwise, with progress and Cancel after half a second, and a free-space check); the original folder is never written. *Hard links and the OneDrive check Windows-unverified.*
- ✓ **Wallpapers and moving backgrounds**: 16 generated wallpapers (mesh gradients, waves, dunes, northern lights, blueprint, …; no image files), blur for wallpapers and custom images, and 6 **animated gradients** with adjustable speed.
- ✓ **Device frames**: browser window (address bar with your title), laptop, monitor and phone, light or dark, vector-drawn so they stay crisp; they shrink with the card when the camera pulls back.
- ✓ **Card looks**: border (solid or frosted *glass*), shadow styles soft / hard (offset, no blur) / glow (coloured), with a colour.
- ✓ **Watermark or logo**: an image (PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG) or text in a corner or the centre, with size, opacity and margin; unaffected by zoom. Changing the logo can be undone.
- ✓ **Image overlays and stickers** (Images tab and track): PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG and animated GIF images (25 MB at most, SVG 2 MB, checked by their content), and 12 built-in vector stickers. Add one with *Add image…*, by dropping a file on the editor or pasting one (`Ctrl+V` outside text fields: an image copied after the last *Copy* of items is pasted as an image, else the copied items are); it goes in at the playhead, 3 s long. Images join multi-select groups like text and shapes (an Images section in the Selection view; copy, paste and duplicate, an image file only within its own recording). Move it on the preview, resize it from its handles (`Shift` keeps its shape), rotate it from the round handle (`Shift`: 15° steps), nudge it with the arrow keys; set opacity and fade in/out in the panel; move and trim it on the track like text. GIFs play frame-exact in the export on the video's own clock; SVGs are only ever drawn as pictures, never run. Images are copied into the project folder (an identical one is stored once), so the project renders the same on another PC; one that is missing is left out and the panel and export dialog say so.
- ✓ **Brand fonts**: a Font menu on text, title cards and captions with *Add font…* (TTF, OTF or WOFF2, 15 MB at most). The font is copied into the project and loaded from its bytes under the app's content security policy; preview and export wait for it and lay it out the same. A font that is missing or cannot be read is drawn with Segoe UI, and the menu and the export dialog say so.
- ✓ **Brand kit** (Settings › Brand kit; Style tab › *Apply brand kit*): your logo (position, size, opacity, distance from the edge), up to 6 colours, up to 6 fonts with a heading and a text font, an intro and an outro title card, and a style preset, edited in one place. *Apply brand kit* applies all of it to the open video as one undo step: the preset's look, the logo as the watermark, the fonts on captions and on text and titles that use the default font, the intro and outro (applying again replaces them), and the colours first in a title card's colour pickers. With *Apply to new recordings* on, every new recording starts with it.
- ✓ **Style presets**: 8 built-in looks (Studio, Minimal, Midnight browser, Neon glow, Pop, Laptop showcase, Frosted glass, Flow) that set background, layout, frame, border, shadow, cursor look and zoom style in one undoable step; save your own looks by name, rename and delete them.
- ✓ **Shapes**: arrow, line, box, ellipse and highlighter, with colour, stroke width, fill and opacity. They are pinned to the content, so they follow zooms and pans. Add from the Shapes tab, the timeline or the command palette, drag and resize on the preview (arrow ends too), move and trim on their own timeline track.
- ✓ **Privacy blur and pixelate**: hide passwords, emails or faces. Fully applied for the whole range (no fade), under the cursor, following the content through zooms, and covering every motion-blur sub-frame.
- ✓ **Spotlight**: dim everything except an ellipse or box that follows the cursor or stays on a region, with darkness and soft edges.
- ✓ **Audio**: master volume and mute; separate microphone and system-sound volume and mute when the recording kept them apart; noise reduction (applied on export); *even out loudness* (about −16 LUFS); fade in / fade out; **background music** (MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, OGG, Opus, FLAC) with volume, loop, fades and automatic lowering while the recording has sound (with the speech detector: while you talk or your PC plays sound; the same ducking in preview and export).
- ✓ **Clean voice** (Audio tab › Voice, F-08 / NW-10): one switch removes fan hum, keyboard and mouse clicks and room noise from the microphone, offline on this PC (ffmpeg's RNNoise filter with two bundled models, about 0.6 MB, nothing downloaded). *Light*, *Normal* or *Strong*; the cleaned voice is made once per project and strength (about 100× real time, with progress and *Cancel*), kept in the project folder, and made again only when the microphone file or the strength changes. The preview plays the cleaned voice (what you hear is what you export), with *Hear the original* for a quick comparison. A recording without separate sources cleans its mixed track (the panel says so). Projects with the switch off, and older projects, sound and export exactly as before. *Windows-unverified.*
- ✓ **Command palette** (`Ctrl+K` or `Ctrl+Shift+P`): search every editor command (playback, editing, zooms, text, shapes, spotlight, toggles, inspector tabs, export, smart speed, ignoring clicks, straightening the cursor), each shown with its shortcut; recently used commands come first.
- ✓ **Custom shortcuts** (`Ctrl+/`): rebind any command, see and resolve conflicts, reset one or all; hints across the editor follow your keys.
- ✓ **Smart speed**: finds stretches where the picture does not change, nothing is heard *and* you did not move, click, scroll or type, then speeds them up (2×/4×/8×) or cuts them in one undoable step, showing how much time it saves.
- ✓ Webcam bubble: show/hide, corner, size, circle, rounded or square, border (width, colour), mirror, drop shadow, and framing (zoom into the picture and drag to choose the visible part).
- ✓ **Background blur behind you** on the camera, fully offline: person segmentation (MediaPipe selfie segmenter) runs in a worker with the model and WebAssembly shipped inside the app; the same mask is used in preview and export. If it cannot run (for example without WebGL) the camera is shown unblurred and the Camera panel says why. *Windows-unverified.*
- ✓ **Camera layouts over time**: screen with bubble, camera only, screen only, side by side, or a large camera, on their own timeline track with smooth 0.4 s transitions (only for recordings with a camera).
- ✓ **Camera position over time**: the camera's corner can change during the video (from the bubble's moves while recording, or set in the Camera tab); corner changes sit on the camera track with the layouts and animate like them.
- ✓ **Automatic camera director** (Camera tab, *Auto director*, or the command *Direct the camera automatically*): one click builds the camera track. The full camera (or a large camera) while you talk before and after the demo, the bubble moved to a corner clear of your cursor and clicks (measured where the video draws them, zooms included), the camera hidden in long silences (5 / 10 / 20 s or never), and your face centred by a static framing found with the bundled person segmentation (no extra model). The plan is shown before it is applied (a strip of the video, each change with its reason, what Direct again would change); applying is one undo step. Its blocks are marked *auto* on the camera track; changing one makes it yours and Direct again keeps it; your recorded camera positions are kept, and *Remove automatic changes* gives them back whole. *Windows-unverified.*
- ✓ **Auto captions**: transcribed from the recording's sound by your own OpenAI or Gemini key (spoken language or auto-detect; the microphone alone when it was recorded separately). Long recordings are sent in parts, cut in pauses where possible, and joined so each word appears once. Progress and Cancel in the Captions tab.
- ✓ **Captions on this PC** (F-01, with the minimal model manager of F-13): free, private captions with exact word timings and no key, no upload and no cost, from Whisper (whisper.cpp 1.8.3 in the `ssw-speech` helper, run at below-normal priority, one short-lived process per part of at most 10 minutes). The Captions tab offers *On this PC (free, private)* first, with plain quality tiers (Fast, Standard, Accurate, and Most accurate once downloaded) labelled with their size or *Downloaded*, and the chosen one's time for this recording; the first use downloads the chosen model (44, 60 or 190 MB; Most accurate, 574 MB, is downloaded in Settings) only after a click that names its size, from a pinned Hugging Face commit, resumable, retried, verified by SHA-256, then makes the captions by itself. Progress in recording time with the time left and the newest words heard; Cancel keeps the finished parts and *Generate captions* continues from them, also after a restart. Karaoke, the caption track, transcript editing, subtitle files and the AI text tools work unchanged. Settings › *Captions on this PC* lists the models (download, cancel, continue, remove, the model used for new captions, *Show folder*), the speech engine (with a slower compatible mode for processors without AVX2) and the speech detector. While you edit elsewhere, a ring on the Captions tab shows the download or the captions running, and a notice says when the captions are ready or the download or the captions failed. Cloud errors offer *Use On this PC*. About › *Third-party notices* shows the licences. Speed, memory, the compatible build, non-ASCII user names, Smart App Control and proxies are *Windows-unverified* (manual checks in `docs/design/offline-captions.md` §10.5).
- ✓ **Bangla captions on this PC** (`docs/design/offline-captions.md` §14): a Bangla-only model, Bengali.AI's Whisper medium (539 MB, Apache-2.0, pinned by commit and SHA-256), free and offline once downloaded. With *Spoken language* Bengali the Captions tab adds a *Bangla* quality chip, chosen by default, and recommends it with one button, *Download Bangla model (539 MB) and make captions*, saying why (it gets about 4 in 5 Bangla words right, where the other models get most wrong; measured 18 % WER on FLEURS Bangla long-form against 98 % for large-v3 turbo) and that it works offline. Once downloaded, Bengali uses it (with 20 s chunks and no timestamp tokens; word timings stay exact; each chunk's text is cut at the speaker's pauses into captions of at most 7 s and 14 words, so subtitle files get short cues); other languages and *Detect automatically* never do, and it never becomes the model for new captions. Captions detected as Bengali with another model offer it in their summary; with it downloaded and *Detect automatically* chosen, *Speaking Bangla? Choose Bengali* switches in one click. Settings lists it as *Bangla only*. Polish in one click uses it when the Captions tab's spoken language is Bengali. Like Most accurate it needs an 8 GB PC with AVX2, and it is slower than the recording on most 4-core PCs (about 29 min for 20 min, said before starting). Captions have no punctuation. Windows speed and memory are *Windows-unverified*.
- ✓ **Captions after OpenAI retires whisper-1** (NW-09, `docs/design/transcription.md`): the suggested OpenAI transcription model is `gpt-transcribe` (OpenAI removes `whisper-1` on 26 February 2027). It gives text without times, so Flown sends 30 s parts cut in pauses and spreads each part's words over where the recording has sound: no word lands in a pause, and every word is marked *estimated*. The Captions tab says so before you generate and in the summary, with *Use On this PC* for exact word times; the Transcript tab underlines estimated words with a dotted line. Gemini's own word times are checked and replaced by estimates where they are impossible. After a model's retirement day a failed request says plainly what to choose instead (*"OpenAI retired whisper-1 on 26 February 2027. Choose gpt-transcribe …"*), not the API's error.
- ✓ **Speaker labels** (NW-09): Gemini labels who speaks ("Speaker 1", "Speaker 2"; kept when there are at least two). A recording that kept the microphone and the computer sound apart gets *You* and *Others* with no AI (Captions tab › Speakers › *Label You and Others*). Rename a speaker once and every caption follows (one undo step); each caption row has a speaker menu; the Transcript tab names the speaker where it changes. WebVTT files carry voice tags (`<v Priya>`); *Speaker names in SRT and text* adds "Priya: "; *Show speaker names* (Look, off by default) draws the name before the caption on the video, in preview and export. Older projects open unchanged.
- ✓ **Caption editing and styles**: a transcript editor (fix text, remove a caption, one undo step each), captions on their own timeline track (move, trim), styles *clean, bold, boxed, karaoke* (each word lights up as it is spoken) and *minimal*, position, size, text and highlight colours, background, and one or two lines. Right-to-left languages are laid out right to left. The same drawing in preview and export.
- ✓ **AI text tools** on the transcript, with OpenAI, Gemini or Claude: clean-up (punctuation and obvious mistakes, timings kept), translate, chapters, a title and a summary. Only the transcript text is sent.
- ✓ **Subtitle files**: SRT, VTT or plain text, following the edit's cuts and speed changes, without the words the Transcript tab shows struck through.
- ✓ **Edit by text** (NW-04, the *Transcript* tab, `docs/design/edit-by-text.md`): the spoken words from the captions, word by word. Select words (click, drag, `Shift`+click, the arrow keys) and press `Delete` to cut that part of the video; cut words stay struck through and *Restore* brings them back. A word whose time is only estimated (captions without word timings, or an edited word) is struck through when you delete it here, or when everything around it is cut, so a cut on the timeline never takes a word that may still be heard out of the subtitles. Clicking a word moves the playhead there, and the word being said is underlined while the video plays. Cuts are ordinary cuts (timeline, preview, captions, chapters and export follow), padded into the pauses so syllables are not clipped, one undo step each. No transcript yet: *Make captions* runs the Captions tab's *Generate captions*.
- ✓ **Remove fillers and retakes** (NW-01): *Find fillers and retakes* lists "um", "uh", "er", "you know" and "like" (the last two only when set apart by a pause or a comma; short lists for Bangla, German, French and Spanish), repeated words, and sentences said again (the last take is kept), with *Keep* / *Remove* each and *Remove all* (one undo step). Nothing is cut until you choose. Found on this PC from the transcript, with no AI call.
- ✓ **Title cards**: centered, big or lower-third titles with an optional subtitle, over a blurred frame, a solid colour or the project's background, with fades; on their own timeline track, edited in the Text panel (`Shift+T` adds one).
- ✓ **Chapters**: flags on the timeline (`M` adds one, `Shift+PageDown` / `Shift+PageUp` jump between them), listed in the Text panel, written into MP4 / MOV / WebM / M4A exports, and **Copy chapters** gives YouTube timestamps with hints when YouTube would not accept them.
- ✓ **Remove silences** (Clip tab, Smart speed): finds stretches without sound (with the speech detector: without your voice or PC sound), keeps a little padding, and cuts or speeds them up in one undoable step. Both smart speed modes leave the time of title cards alone.
- ✓ **Speech detector** (F-03): Silero VAD v6.2.0 (885 KB, MIT) ships in the installer and runs once per recording (about 9 s per hour of microphone sound), its result kept in the project. On the microphone, Remove silences, Smart speed, music ducking and the camera director listen for your voice, so typing, clicks and fans no longer count as sound (a short tail around each word keeps soft endings); sound your PC plays still counts, a recording without a separate microphone counts speech and any other sound (never less than before), and, except for the camera director, a microphone with almost no speech but much sound (a typing tutorial) is judged by its level. The Clip tab says which rule ran (nothing when only PC sound is heard, the microphone muted or never recorded: then nothing changes). Without the detector everything works exactly as before. **Existing projects' music ducking changes at their next export** (it ducks under speech, not under typing).
- ✓ **Polish in one click** (the Polish button, `Shift+P` or the command palette): runs your style preset, auto zoom, Remove silences, captions and the camera director in one go, with progress, Skip for the slow parts and Cancel. Captions are made on this PC once a speech model is downloaded (no key needed; with the Bangla model when the Captions tab's spoken language is Bengali), else with your AI provider that can transcribe; the dialog lets you pick between them. Polish never starts a download, and with *On this PC* chosen but no model downloaded it leaves captions out rather than upload the sound. The result is one undo step and a summary (how much shorter, cuts, zooms, camera changes, captions) with *Undo polish*. Your own zooms, camera blocks, captions, title cards, crop and freeze frames are kept; the options are remembered.
- ✓ Audio volume (0–200%) and mute, applied to preview and export.
- ✓ Zoomable, scrollable timeline (`+`/`-`, `Ctrl`+wheel, Fit) that stays usable for long recordings; playhead follows playback.
- ✓ **Audio waveform on the timeline**: the sound drawn on the clip track (microphone above and system sound below when they were recorded apart, grey when muted), with pauses shaded, following cuts and speed. On by default; *Audio waveform on the timeline* in the command palette hides it.
- ✓ **Snapping**: item edges, chapter flags, trim handles, range ends and the scrubbed playhead snap to cuts, clicks, chapters, freeze frames, the playhead and other items while dragging, with a labelled guide; hold `Alt` to place freely; the magnet button or `N` turns it off. A drag that lands back on the item's own place adds no undo step.
- ✓ **Select several items and edit them together** (E-06): `Ctrl`+click or `Shift`+click blocks on the timeline (a run on one track), `Shift`+drag a box across the overlay tracks (`Ctrl`+drag adds), `Ctrl+A` for the track you last used (again: every item), `Ctrl`+click items on the paused preview, or `Ctrl+Space` in the Inspector's item lists; `Esc` clears. Zooms, text, shapes, blur and pixelate, spotlights, title cards, chapters and freeze frames can be selected together; captions, camera layouts, clips and recorded clicks stay one at a time, and a `Ctrl`+click on one says why and keeps the group. Selected blocks show an outline and a check, and the timeline header counts them. Dragging a selected block moves them all with their spacing kept, with snapping, stopping at the recording's ends, at zooms in the way and where a freeze frame would leave its clip. The Inspector's **Selection** view lists them (each can be left out), moves them (Starts at, ±1 s), sets a shared Length, and edits what they share per kind (zoom level, focus, easing, transition; text style, size, position; shape colour, fill, line width, opacity; blur amount; spotlight shape, darkness, soft edge; title card style and colours; freeze length and sound), with *Mixed* where they differ. `Delete`, `Ctrl+X` / `Ctrl+C` / `Ctrl+V` (pasted at the playhead), `Ctrl+D` (a copy right after them) and `Ctrl+Shift+C` / `Ctrl+Shift+V` (copy one item's look, paste it onto the selected items of its kind; also as buttons in each item's panel). Every group action is one undo step; automatic zooms become yours when moved or edited, as single edits do. Items are copied inside Flown only, never to the Windows clipboard; nothing is added to `project.json`. `Ctrl+A/C/X/V` with Electron's Edit menu, the look keys on AZERTY and Polish keyboards, touchpad boxes and 500-item drags are *Windows-unverified* (`docs/design/multi-select.md` §10.5).
- ✓ Shortcuts work regardless of which control was last used, and are paused behind the export dialog. Sharp preview on high-DPI screens; only redraws when something changed.
- ✓ Auto-save with a save indicator; save failures are shown with Retry; edits are flushed when closing the window or going back.
- ✓ **Save as default style**: background, layout, frame, border, shadow, watermark, cursor, camera, effect settings and the zoom style become the starting look of every new recording (Reset restores the built-in look).
- ✓ Projects library on the home screen (open / delete / show in Explorer).
- ✓ **Import a video** made with another app (OBS, Xbox Game Bar, Teams, a phone): *Import video* on Home, or drop the file on *Recent recordings*. MP4, MOV, MKV and WebM with H.264, HEVC, VP8, VP9, AV1 and whatever else the bundled ffmpeg decodes. It is checked first (content, size, length up to 4 hours, picture size, copy protection, a test decode) and refused with one plain sentence when it cannot be edited; then an editing copy is made (constant 30 or 60 fps, upright, square pixels, at most 3840 × 2160; HDR tone-mapped, interlaced video deinterlaced) with progress, time left and Cancel. Your file is never changed. Everything in the editor works except what needs the cursor, clicks or keys Flown records (auto zoom, the smooth cursor, click effects and sounds, the shortcut overlay, cursor-following items); those say why. Manual zooms work as usual. (`docs/design/import-video.md`; HDR filters and GPU encoders *Windows-unverified*.)

## Export
- ✓ MP4 (H.264 + AAC) at 720p / 1080p / 1440p / 4K, 30 or 60 fps, quality presets (Studio / Web / Small file); a warning when exporting above the recording's resolution.
- ✓ **GPU-accelerated** when available: frames are decoded in order with WebCodecs and encoded with WebCodecs or ffmpeg's NVENC / Quick Sync / AMF, falling back to x264 (or choose *Software*). Progress shows fps and ETA. *GPU paths Windows-unverified.*
- ✓ Exact frames (no duplicated or skipped frames) and correct, tagged colours. Very wide or tall areas are capped to encoder limits (the dialog shows the real size).
- ✓ Audio follows cuts and speed changes and stays in sync; separate sources, noise reduction, loudness, fades, music and click sounds are mixed in one ffmpeg pass (M4A includes all of it; GIF has no sound). Projects without the new audio settings export exactly as before.
- ✓ **Smooth cuts** (NW-06, audio): the recording's sound fades out over 10 ms before every cut and back in over 10 ms after it, in the export and in the preview, so a cut never clicks. Music and click sounds are not affected. *Later*: a short video cross-dissolve and per-cut transition styles.
- ✓ GIF with bounded memory (palette passes), up to 1080p / 15 fps.
- ✓ Safe output: written to a temporary file and moved into place only when complete; cancel works in every phase and never destroys an existing file.
- ✓ **Export bundle** (F-11): *Also save* writes the captions as SRT and VTT, a chapter list (YouTube timestamps), the thumbnail (the chosen frame at the export size) and a description (title, length, chapters) next to the video, named after it (`Demo.srt`, `Demo - chapters.txt`, …). A file of the same name is never replaced unless the export replaced the video itself; otherwise " (2)" is added. **One file per chapter** exports each chapter into a folder as `01 Intro.mp4`, `02 Billing.mp4`, …, with the same frames as that part of the whole export, each with its own captions; music starts again in each file.
- ✓ **Hands-off long jobs** (F-12): exports, captions on this PC and sound analysis show progress on the taskbar button (paused in yellow, red after a failure); the export can be paused in the dialog or from Pause / Stop on the taskbar thumbnail; the PC is kept from sleeping while a job runs (the screen may still turn off) and the export goes on when the screen is locked; if the PC sleeps anyway the export pauses and carries on by itself after it wakes; when Flown is in the background an "Export finished" notification offers Open and Show in folder. Thumbnail buttons and notification buttons are *Windows-unverified*.
- ✓ **Copy** the exported file to the clipboard (paste into Explorer, Slack, Discord, …) and *Show in folder*.
- ✓ Tall and square exports of a project that follows the action (the TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Square post presets, and batch items) show the frame exactly as previewed; a 16:9 export of the same project shows the whole screen as before, so one batch can make a classic and a followed video. The dialog says how much the recording is magnified.
- ✓ **Destination presets** (YouTube 1080p/4K, X, LinkedIn, Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Square, Slack GIF, Email) that set format, size, frame rate, aspect (export only) and size limits; **batch export** of several presets into a folder; **target file size** (two-pass); **MP4 / MOV / WebM (VP9) / GIF / M4A**; **PNG thumbnail** of any frame.

## App health
- ✓ **Storage** (Settings, F-26): disk use per recording (largest first, with its cache) and in total, with the free space on the drive. Clean-up rules: clear the caches of recordings not used for 14–90 days (by hand, or automatically at most once a day), and remove recordings not used for 30–365 days. *Review clean-up…* lists every item first; only the ticked ones move, always to the Recycle Bin, never outside the projects folder, never the open or an unfinished recording.
- ✓ **Diagnostics** (Settings, X-03): *Show diagnostics* shows a report (Windows build, displays, GPU and encoders, the capture helper's probe, how the last recording was made, running jobs, recent app messages) and *Copy diagnostics* copies exactly it. The app keeps a small log in its settings folder (at most 2 MB, *Open log folder*); keys, tokens, e-mail addresses and paths outside the app's own folders are taken out before anything is written.
